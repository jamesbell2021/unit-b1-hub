Panel pack — staff and student panellists

Running the mock interviews

A three-person panel interviews each candidate for the Anchorlight Games trainee role. Everyone gets the same six core questions, in the same order, scored against the same six criteria. That's what makes it fair, and it's what makes the feedback worth having.

At 20 minutes a candidate, plan for three an hour. A class of 15 needs about five hours, so spread it across two sessions and rotate who sits on the panel.

Ground rules for student panellists

  • Same questions for everyone. Don't drop hints to friends or skip questions for anyone.
  • Score on what you heard, not on who the person is or how well you know them.
  • Score on your own first, then agree with the panel. Disagreement is normal. Talk it through.
  • Keep it confidential. Don't discuss anyone's scores outside the room.
  • Stay in role. Professional, friendly and no laughing. Treat it as if it were real.
  • Feedback is specific and kind: "Your portfolio walkthrough was clear because…", not "you were good".

Panellists get evidence too

Sitting on the panel, writing notes and giving structured feedback is good AC5 Communication evidence for the two student panellists. They should write it up in their reflective logs: what they looked for, and what they learned about interviews from the other side of the table.

Room set-up

  • Waiting area outside Candidates wait outside and are collected, as at a real interview. Arrival and manner count towards the professional presence score.
  • Panel on one side, candidate opposite Water for everyone. Space to show a laptop or phone.
  • Recording Camera behind the panel, on the candidate. Record only with the candidate's consent, and give them an alternative if they decline.

02 — Questions

Six core questions, in this order

Ask them as written. Use the probes only if an answer is thin. The "listen for" notes tell you what a strong answer contains.

Spare questions

Use one if a candidate finishes very quickly, or swap one in for a candidate with a different track. Use the same swap for everyone in that track.

03 — Scoring

Six criteria, scored 1 to 4

Score each criterion separately. The total is out of 24. The bands on the right are a guide to how the performance would read against the AC5 grade descriptors. The final grade decision stays with the assessor, across all the B1 evidence.
